    What to box

    The team's convention. Ask the lead mentor if unsure.

    • Every robot you can see: one box around the whole visible robot, bumpers included, tight to its edges.
    • Partly hidden (behind the hub, a referee or another robot): box the part you can see.
    • Cut off at the edge of the frame: box it up to the edge.
    • Never box as a robot: people, carts, field structures (hub, walls, corrals) or the team icons in the score overlay.
    • Not sure about robots off the field (the queue line) or close-ups from the broadcast? Ask the lead mentor, or Skip the frame.

    The buttons

    Accept A
    Saves the boxes you see. Nothing to box? Accept, then confirm “No robots here” with N.
    Skip S
    Come back later: saves nothing, the frame stays in the queue (it comes back when you reload).
    Bad B
    Replay, graphics, black or unusable: saved as bad and kept out of training. Undo in the message, or go back and Accept.
    • Auto: the models' boxes, tagged with their confidence (.73)
    • Guess: a bumper-colour proposal. Not saved unless you keep it (K, the Keep button, or move it)
    • Solid: reviewed, imported, or drawn by you
